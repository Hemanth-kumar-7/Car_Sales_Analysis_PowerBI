# 🚗 Car Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI Project

## Overview  
This project focuses on analyzing car sales data to uncover key insights related to sales performance, regional trends, dealer activity, and customer preferences.  

The interactive dashboard was built using Power BI, transforming raw Excel data into a visually engaging and data-driven report that supports strategic business decision-making.  

It demonstrates proficiency in data cleaning, DAX, data modeling, visualization, and storytelling — essential skills for data analysts and business intelligence professionals.  

---

## Project Objectives  
The primary goals of this project are to:  
- Track total sales, average price, and total cars sold on a Year-to-Date (YTD) and Month-to-Date (MTD) basis.  
- Analyze sales performance by dealer, company, and region.  
- Understand customer preferences based on car body style and color.  
- Identify sales trends across weekly, monthly, and yearly timelines.  
- Generate insights to support sales growth and optimize inventory management strategies.  

---

## Dashboard Features  

### KPI Highlights  
- **YTD Total Sales:** $371.2M  
- **YTD Average Price:** $28.0K  
- **YTD Cars Sold:** 13.3K  
- **MTD vs YTD Growth Metrics:** Provides a clear comparison of monthly and yearly progress.  

### Key Visuals  
- **Sales Trend:** Weekly performance trends displayed in a line chart.  
- **Sales by Body Style:** Comparison of popular car types such as SUV, Hatchback, Sedan, Passenger, and Hardtop.  
- **Sales by Color:** Visualization of customer preferences based on car color.  
- **Cars Sold by Dealer Region:** Ranking of top-performing sales regions.  
- **Company-Wise Sales Trend:** Comparison of total sales, average price, and cars sold across different car brands.  

### Interactive Filters  
Users can interact with the dashboard using dynamic filters such as:  
- Body Style  
- Dealer Name  
- Transmission Type  
- Engine Type  

---

## Key Insights  
- SUVs and Sedans emerged as the most popular body styles, contributing significantly to total revenue.  
- Black and Red cars were the most preferred colors among buyers, accounting for a large portion of total sales.  
- Austin and Janesville were identified as top-performing regions in terms of sales.  
- Cadillac and Lexus recorded the highest average car prices among all brands.  
- Sales activity peaked around mid-year, reflecting seasonal buying patterns and promotional impacts.  

---

## Business Recommendations  
- Focus marketing campaigns on the most popular car colors and body styles to maximize conversions.  
- Offer targeted discounts or promotional campaigns in underperforming regions to stimulate demand.  
- Strengthen partnerships with high-performing dealers to maintain momentum and consistency.  
- Monitor brand-level pricing trends to fine-tune promotional and pricing strategies.  
- Use historical sales data to implement inventory forecasting models and ensure optimal stock levels.

## Tools and Technologies Used  
- **Data Source:** Microsoft Excel   
- **BI Tool:** Power BI   
- **Data Preparation:** Power Query  
- **DAX Calculations:** Year-to-Date (YTD), Month-to-Date (MTD), and Percentage Growth metrics  
- **Visualizations:** KPIs, Line Charts, Bar Charts, and Donut Charts  
- **Skills Demonstrated:** Data Cleaning, Data Modeling, DAX, Business Analysis, and Data Storytelling  

---

## Project Workflow  
1. **Data Collection:** Imported raw car sales data from Excel into Power BI.  
2. **Data Cleaning:** Removed null values, standardized formats, and corrected inconsistencies for data accuracy.  
3. **Data Modeling:** Established relationships between the dealer, car, and sales tables.  
4. **DAX Calculations:** Created key performance measures including YTD, MTD, and growth metrics.  
5. **Visualization:** Designed an interactive and visually appealing Power BI dashboard with filters and slicers.  
6. **Insight Generation:** Interpreted key patterns to generate actionable business recommendations.
